FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does Thread save?▾

The state of your argument, not just your files: which claims feel settled, which are in tension, what's still open — and the exact question you were holding when you stopped.

How is this different from Notion, Obsidian, or Roam?▾

Those tools keep what you wrote. Thread keeps where you were. Content survives fine in any tool — it's the thread of the argument that disappears between sessions, and that thread is the product.

Is it for writing or for code?▾

Both. Claims, tensions, and open questions describe a design doc or a debugging journal as well as they describe a chapter. If the work spans sessions, it has a thread worth keeping.

Where does my thinking live?▾

In your browser, in the current version — with one-click JSON export and import. Nothing is locked in.
