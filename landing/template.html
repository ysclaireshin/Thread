Never lose your thread.

Step away mid-thought. Come back to the exact sentence that starts you moving again — instead of spending an hour re-reading your own notes just to remember where you were.

Thread keeps a live visual map of your thinking — the unfinished threads, open questions, and claims — so the moment you return, where you left off is already in front of you.

↓

Most people who work on long projects don't fail from a lack of ideas. They stall because every time they stop, their line of reasoning slips away. Coming back means piecing together connections, questions, and claims all over again before you can move forward.
